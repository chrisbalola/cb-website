About

Most software skips most of the world. We don't.

Most software is built for people with fast internet, new phones and a credit card. Emigisha builds for everyone else too. Our products support the things big tools tend to skip: mobile money, low connectivity, local languages.

The name comes from Mashi, the language of the Bashi people of eastern Congo. It means blessings, and it is the bar we hold our work to.

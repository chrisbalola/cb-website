About

Most software skips most of the world.

Most software is built for people with fast internet, new phones and a credit card. Emigisha builds for everyone else too: mobile money, low connectivity, local languages.

The name comes from Mashi, the language of the Bashi people of eastern Congo. We are a small remote team. We ship often and keep things simple.

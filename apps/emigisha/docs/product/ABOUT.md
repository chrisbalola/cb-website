# Emigisha

## About

Emigisha, LLC is a small company founded by Christian Balola. It builds software for people who run businesses, and it is the legal entity that operates Meneja, Talatala and StyledDoc.

The website exists so that customers, app stores, payment providers and partners who look the company up find a real, accountable company and what it makes.

## Products

### Meneja

Finance, products and services management in one app.

- Website: [about.meneja.app](https://about.meneja.app)
- Status: live

### Talatala

Studio photos from a selfie, paid with mobile money.

- Website: [talatala.app](https://talatala.app)
- Status: live

### StyledDoc

Designed documents from plain content.

- Website: [styleddoc.app](https://styleddoc.app)
- Status: building

## What the site says

- "Emigisha means blessings." The name is the standard the work is held to.

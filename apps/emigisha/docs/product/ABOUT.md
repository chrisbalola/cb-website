# Emigisha

## About

Emigisha, LLC is a small company founded by Christian Balola. It builds software for people who run businesses, and it is the legal entity that operates Meneja.

The website exists so that customers, app stores, payment providers and partners who look the company up find a real, accountable company and what it makes.

# Chris Balola — Indie Hacker

## About

Chris Balola is an indie hacker building software products. The personal website at [chrisbalola.com](https://chrisbalola.com) serves as a hub for showcasing projects, sharing the building journey, and connecting with the community.

## Products

### Meneja (First Product)

Meneja (Swahili for "manage") is an all-in-one management platform designed for individuals, small businesses, and organizations. It brings together finance, products, and services management into a single app. Built with clean architecture, offline-first capabilities, and multi-currency support.

- **Website**: [meneja.app](https://meneja.app)
- **Status**: Live (Finance module)
